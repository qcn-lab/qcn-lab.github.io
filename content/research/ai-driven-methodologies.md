We develop and apply artificial intelligence and machine learning to scientific discovery and quantum control.

We aim to use AI to analyze complex scientific data, uncover physical principles, design and control quantum systems, accelerate scientific computation, and automate experiments.

Our research combines physics-informed learning, inverse design, and autonomous experimentation, with the goal of developing AI methods that can work closely with both theoretical models and experimental platforms.

Our long-term vision is to build increasingly autonomous scientific workflows in which AI, theory, and experiment interact in a closed loop, accelerating the discovery of new physics and the development of new quantum technologies.
