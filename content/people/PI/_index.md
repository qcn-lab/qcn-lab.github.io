Xiangjin Kong is a Research Fellow at the Institute of Modern Physics, Fudan University. His research focuses on quantum interactions between X-ray photons and atomic nuclei, with particular emphasis on nuclear quantum optics, X-ray photonic structures, and quantum control of nuclear systems.

With the development of advanced X-ray light sources, including synchrotron radiation facilities and X-ray free-electron lasers, studies of light–matter interactions have been extended into the X-ray and nuclear regimes. These advances provide new opportunities for exploring quantum optical phenomena involving nuclear transitions.

His research aims to develop effective approaches for the quantum control of X-ray photons and nuclear states by combining theoretical studies with experiments based on advanced X-ray sources. Potential applications include extending quantum information science toward shorter wavelengths, overcoming limitations associated with the optical diffraction limit, and developing high-precision nuclear clocks based on nuclear transitions.
