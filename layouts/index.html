ABOUT THE GROUP

Welcome to QCN Research Group

Our research group explores the frontiers of quantum optics and light–matter interactions along three complementary directions: nuclear quantum optics, quantum-dot experimental platforms, and AI-driven methodologies.

We are committed to advancing quantum science and training the next generation of researchers.

Led by Prof. Xiangjin Kong, our team integrates expertise in theoretical quantum optics, experimental photonics, and artificial intelligence to push the boundaries of quantum optics.

RESEARCH

Research Areas

01

Nuclear Quantum Optics

Exploring quantum optical phenomena, collective nuclear excitations, and light–matter interactions in nuclear systems.

02

Quantum-Dot Experimental Platforms

Developing quantum-dot based experimental platforms for investigating quantum optical effects and controlling light–matter interactions.

03

AI-Driven Methodologies

Integrating artificial intelligence with quantum science for modeling, optimization, automated discovery, and intelligent experimental design.
