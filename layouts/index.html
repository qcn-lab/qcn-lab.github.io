ABOUT THE GROUP

Exploring the Frontiers of Quantum Science

Led by Prof. Xiangjin Kong and Prof. Yugang Ma, the Quantum Control of Nuclei (QCN) Group investigates fundamental quantum phenomena and develops new approaches for controlling complex quantum systems.

Our research centers on three directions: quantum control of nuclei using synchrotron radiation and XFELs, quantum physics under low-temperature and high-magnetic-field conditions, and AI for Science.

By integrating theory, advanced experiments, and artificial intelligence, we aim to expand the frontiers of quantum science and enable new capabilities in quantum control, discovery, and technology.

Research Areas

Quantum Control of Nuclei
