Group Philosophy

Our group aims to cultivate four core qualities in our students: Courage, Endeavor, Faith, and Balance (CEFB).

Courage to pursue ambitious goals, embrace uncertainty, and face challenges.

Endeavor to work toward those goals with sustained effort, dedication, and perseverance.

Faith in yourself, in the value of your efforts, and in the power of time—especially when progress is slow and setbacks arise.

Balance research and life, remembering that study and research are parts of a fulfilling life, not its entirety.
